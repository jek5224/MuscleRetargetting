# Semitendinosus reference-surface selection

The immutable geometric reference selected for full-muscle reconstruction is
`tet_orig_std/L_Semitendinosus_tet.npz`, specifically its `render_faces`
surface. It has 6,786 vertices and 13,568 triangles and is a single,
watertight, consistently oriented two-manifold component. Its bounds match the
current simulation asset in metre units. Against the current tet boundary its
bidirectional RMS distance is approximately 0.95 mm and its maximum distance
is approximately 5.76 mm.

The reference is copied into generated outputs but is never modified in place.
The repaired simulation surface uses the reference geometry and connectivity;
the invalid current tet-boundary connectivity is not retained.

Alternatives were rejected as the primary reference:

- `tet/L_Semitendinosus_tet.npz`: closest to the failed contact experiment,
  but its extracted boundary contains the proven four-sheet branches.
- `tet_orig_quality_v2/L_Semitendinosus_tet.npz`: manifold, but farther from
  the current contact geometry (about 2.72 mm RMS and 15.8 mm maximum).
- `tet_orig_sim/L_Semitendinosus_tet.npz`: manifold, but farther from the
  current contact geometry (about 3.02 mm RMS and 15.0 mm maximum).
- `Zygote_Meshes_251229/Muscle/UpLeg/L_Semitendinosus.obj`: anatomically
  detailed but open, and therefore unsuitable as the direct simulation
  surface without a separate opening-reconstruction pass.
- `DTI/Subject 01/BuiltMeshes/L_Semitendinosus.obj`: watertight but contains
  two components and uses a different coordinate scale.

Machine-readable rankings and complete topology reports are generated by
`python -m muscle_sim.inspect_full_surface`.
