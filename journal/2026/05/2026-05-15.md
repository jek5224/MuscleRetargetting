## Reverse-LBS solver (feasibility study)

Built `tools/reverse_lbs_solve.py` to recover DART-style LBS rest
positions from baked cache. Goal: replace NN inference with DART's
native LBS path when residuals are acceptable.

Math per waypoint with weights `(w_O, w_I)` (arc-length from rest fiber):

```
P_world(t) = w_O · (R_O(t) @ local_O + t_O(t))
           + w_I · (R_I(t) @ local_I + t_I(t))
```

Stack 132 frames × 3 = 396 equations vs. 6 unknowns. `np.linalg.lstsq`
solves per waypoint. Endpoints get single-bone closed-form.

### 2-bone results

Mono-articular: <5 mm p95 across all (vastus, glutes, adductors).
**Biarticular: catastrophic** — Rectus_Femoris 146 mm, hamstrings
143-145 mm, sartorius 144 mm, gracilis 145 mm. Cause: 2-bone LBS can't
represent fiber wrap around femur for hip-knee biarticular muscles.

### 3-bone extension

Added `solve_three_bone_lbs` + kinematic-chain bone selection. Per
fiber: pick intermediate body from chain whose rest centroid is closest
to fiber midpoint. Tent weights at breakpoint `u_m` (arc fraction where
fiber crosses intermediate at rest), clamped to [0.1, 0.9].

Per-waypoint method selection: compute both 2-bone and 3-bone, keep
lower p95 residual. Avoids regression on monoarticular muscles whose
chain has 1 spurious intermediate (Vastus chain has Tibia between Femur
and Patella but muscle bypasses it anatomically).

### Final residuals (all 74 R+L muscles, 10000 fibers, 100800 waypoints)

- Best (<1 mm p95): glutes, gemelli, obturators
- Most (1-5 mm p95): vasti, adductors, gastroc/soleus, biceps_femoris,
  popliteus
- Worst (5-12 mm p95): sartorius (7.2), gracilis (8.6-11.9),
  semitendinosus (5.8-7.5), rectus_femoris (4.7-6.3), tibialis_anterior
  (5.0-6.2), extensor_hallucis (6.5-6.8), extensor_digitorum (8.0-8.2)
- Max single-waypoint residual: 15.9 mm (Flexor_Digitorum_Longus tail)

**Conclusion**: reverse-LBS is viable for walk gait. Per-waypoint method
tally: 20000 endpoints + 40929 2-bone + 39871 3-bone.
