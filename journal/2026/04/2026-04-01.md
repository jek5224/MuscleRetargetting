# Research Journal — 2026-04-01

## Stream X-Smooth & Z-Smooth Fixes

### X-Smooth
- Anchor-first approach for both stream and pre-cut modes: chain non-square-like levels from anchors, then square-like
- `_best_4rotation`: full 3D frame comparison using trace of rotation matrix, z contribution forced to 1.0
- Removed (1,0,0) init for pre-cut start level — was causing cascading 270° rotations
- Pre-cut x-smooth now uses same anchor-first approach as stream x-smooth

### Z-Smooth
- Global direction + chain propagation + outsider correction
- Only flips basis_z, recomputes basis_y = cross(z, x) — flipping z+x was negating x and breaking alignment
- Handedness fix: recompute basis_y after every z-flip

- Uses raw `self.contours` vertices (not `contour_match` which is 2D-projected)
- Picks best match with adjacent level to determine winding
