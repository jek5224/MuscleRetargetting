# Research Journal — 2026-04-03

## pyuipc Bake: L+R UpLeg Walk (No Contact)

### Multi-Bone LBS
Rewrote the bake script's LBS system to use `cap_attachments` for per-vertex bone assignment:
- Cap anchor vertices rigidly follow their specific bone
- Multi-stream muscles (e.g. Biceps Femoris) use all bones from all streams (Os_Coxae + Femur + Tibia_Fibula)
- Interior vertices: heat-diffused weights on tet mesh adjacency (100 iterations) for smooth blending
- Per-frame Laplacian smoothing of LBS targets (3 iterations) fixes candy-wrapper artifacts

### Bake Results
- 50 muscles (25 L + 25 R UpLeg), walk.bvh, 132 frames
- ~77 seconds on A6000 GPU, 0.58s/frame
- Zero stuck vertices, all cap vertices properly attached
- Smoothness: >5mm variation dropped 90%+ across muscles

### Collision Post-Process

- IPC contact still doesn't converge (SPC vs IPC fight)
