# Research Journal — 2026-03-24

## Inspect 2D Improvements

- **CCW/CW display rotation buttons**: Rotates visual projection of contour (both left Q and right P sides) without modifying data. Useful for comparing adjacent contours with different basis orientations.
- **Reset button**: Re-runs `find_contour_match` to reset correspondence to original. Also recomputes waypoints.
- **Hover preview for correspondence editing**: Click corner → hover vertex to see live preview of updated correspondence and fibers in both 2D and 3D. Click to confirm, Cancel to restore.
- **Stable corner indices**: Stored explicitly in `bp_info` so editing one corner doesn't shift others.
- **3D highlights**: Hovered P vertex shows in 3D viewer. Hovered waypoint highlights entire fiber line in blue.
- **Per-axis normalization on right side**: BP extent fills canvas like left-side unit square.
- **Closest-point hover detection**: Changed from first-match to closest-match.

## Inspect 2D Rotation Investigation

The "topological inconsistency" between adjacent contours in inspect 2D turned out to be a **display artifact** from basis vector orientation differences, not an actual data problem. Corner correspondences and fibers were correct (no fiber twisting). The CCW/CW rotation buttons allow visual confirmation.

Investigated multiple potential causes: basis alignment in `save_bounding_planes`, post-processing in `smoothen_contours` second pass (90° rotation alignment), newell normal consistency. All diagnostics (det, signed area, newell·z) showed consistent data. The visual rotation was purely from the projection basis.
