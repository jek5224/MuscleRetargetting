# Research Journal — 2026-03-11

## L-Side Dance Bake Bug Investigation & Resolution

**Problem**: L-side muscles didn't follow skeleton during dance.bvh playback — they deformed locally but stayed near rest position (Z≈0) while the character moved meters.

**Evidence from cached data**:
- Dance L_Adductor_Brevis: Z stays ±0.08m across all 7829 frames
- Dance R_Adductor_Brevis: Z tracks root motion correctly (−2.83m at frame 500)
- Walk L data: correct (Z=1.39m at frame 131)
- Both L_UpLeg (server bake) and L_LowLeg (local bake) were broken

**Investigation**:
Systematically tested all components in isolation:
- `_update_fixed_targets_from_skeleton()` — produces correct targets when called directly (2.86m at frame 500)
- ARAP backends (CPU, taichi) — don't modify rest_positions in-place
- `setPositions()` + `getWorldTransform()` — skeleton transforms update correctly
- 2-muscle sequential bake (500 frames, CPU) — works
- 2-muscle sequential bake (500 frames, taichi) — works
- 37-muscle direct jump to frame 500 — works
- 37-muscle sequential bake (310 frames, taichi) — works

**Conclusion**: Cannot reproduce with current code. The bad cached data was baked with an older version that had a bug (likely since fixed by intermediate commits). Resolution: rebake L-side data.

**Rebakes started**:
- L_UpLeg: 25 muscles, ~1.33s/frame, ETA ~2.4h
- L_LowLeg: 12 muscles, ~3.28s/frame, ETA ~6.6h

Both showing correct displacement growth (3.0m+ at frames 500-1400), confirming the fix.
